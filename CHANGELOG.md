## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-16

### Added

- Initial release.
- 20 specialist agents covering strategy, engineering, design, marketing, growth, support, finance, operations, security, research and delivery.
- `just-do-it` skill — staffs a team and runs a task through to a finished deliverable.
- `work-with-me` skill — collaborates one unit per turn and hands back for direction.
- `plain-recap` skill — outcome-first reporting for non-technical readers.
